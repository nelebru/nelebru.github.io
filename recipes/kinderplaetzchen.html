Kinderplätzchen (Cut-Out Cookies)

The classic, simple shortcrust dough for cutting out Christmas cookies.
Only five ingredients and easy enough to bake with kids.

Ingredients

  • 250 g flour
  • 150 g butter, cold
  • 75 g sugar
  • 1 packet vanilla sugar (8 g)
  • 1 egg yolk

Instructions

  1. Quickly knead flour, butter in small pieces, sugar, vanilla sugar and egg yolk into a smooth dough.
  2. Wrap the dough and chill for at least 30 minutes.
  3. Preheat the oven to 180°C (160°C fan).
  4. Roll out the dough on a floured surface to about 3–4 mm and cut out cookies.
  5. Bake on a lined baking tray for about 8–10 minutes until the edges are lightly golden.
  6. Let cool completely, then decorate as you like.
