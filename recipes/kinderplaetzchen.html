Kinderplätzchen

Cut-out cookies

The classic, simple shortcrust dough for cutting out Christmas cookies.

Ingredients

  • 250 g flour
  • 150 g butter
  • 75 g sugar
  • 1 packet vanilla sugar (8 g)
  • 1 egg yolk
  • Egg white for brushing, sprinkles, sugar or chocolate for decoration

Instructions

  1. Knead flour, butter in small pieces, sugar, vanilla sugar and egg yolk into a smooth dough.
  2. Wrap the dough and chill for at least 30 minutes.
  3. Preheat the oven to 180°C (160°C fan).
  4. Roll out the dough on a floured surface to about 3–4 mm and cut out cookies.
  5. Place the cookies on a lined baking tray, brush with egg white, decorate with sprinkles, sugar, jam, etc.
  6. Bake on a lined baking tray for about 8–10 minutes until the edges are lightly golden.
  7. Let cool completely and store in an airtight container.
