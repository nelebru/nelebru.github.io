Naan

Fluffy, warm, easy to make in a pan.
Goes well with red lentil dal.

Ingredients

  • 300 g wheat or light spelt flour
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1/4 tsp sugar
  • 1 tsp dry yeast (or 10 g fresh yeast)
  • 100 ml warm milk
  • 100 g plain yogurt
  • 1/2 tsp baking powder
  • Optional: melted butter or ghee for brushing

Instructions

  1. Stir yeast and sugar into the warm milk.
  2. Add flour, yogurt, salt, and baking powder. Knead the dough with a mixer (or by hand) for about 5 minutes until smooth. If needed, add more milk or flour. Cover and let rise in a warm place for 1 hour.
  3. Divide the dough into equal portions, form balls, and stretch or roll each into a small, slightly thicker-edged flatbread. Do not roll too thick.
  4. Heat oil or butter in a large nonstick pan until hot. Place 1–3 naans in the pan. When the bottom of the naan is golden brown, flip and bake the other side until done.
  5. Repeat with remaining dough. Brush with melted butter or ghee to serve.
