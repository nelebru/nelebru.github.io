Carrot Bread

Carrot bread that’s moist and fluffy on the inside, wonderfully crisp on the outside and never fails.

Ingredients

    Dough

  • 300 g spelt flour (Type 630)
  • 200 g wheat flour (Type 1150)
  • 25 g fresh yeast
  • 13 g salt
  • 350 ml warm water
  • 200 g carrots, grated (about 2 medium)
  • 100 g flax seeds, walnuts, or hazelnuts, chopped (optional)

    • Topping (optional)

  • 1 tsp black sesame seeds
  • 1 tsp light sesame seeds
  • 1 tsp flax seeds
  • 1 tsp pumpkin seeds
  • 1 tsp sunflower seeds
  • 1 tsp rolled oats

Instructions

  1. In a large bowl, combine the spelt flour, wheat flour, and salt. Coarsely grate the carrots and stir them in along with the flax seeds, walnuts, or hazelnuts, if using. Dissolve the yeast in the water and add. Mix until no dry flour remains.
  2. Cover the bowl and refrigerate overnight (or at least 1,5h in a warm place), until the dough has doubled in size.
  3. Take the dough out and let it sit at room temperature for 15 minutes. Turn it out onto a floured surface. Fold the edges of the dough toward the center to form a ball, then flip it seam-side down into a floured baking dish (should have a lid).
  4. If using the topping, mix the seeds and oats together, sprinkle evenly over the dough, and press gently. Let rise for about 1 more hour.
  5. Preheat the oven to 230°C (fan).
  6. Bake covered for about 30 minutes. Remove the lid and bake for another 15 minutes, until deeply golden and crisp. Enjoy warm :)
