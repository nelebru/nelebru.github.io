Orzo with Calabrese Pesto

with chickpeas, hidden veggies and halloumi

Creamy orzo cooked with chickpeas, grated carrot and mushrooms,
with a homemade roasted pepper pesto and topped with crispy fried halloumi.

Ingredients

Calabrese pesto

  • 2 red pointed peppers
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 20 g pine nuts (or cashews)
  • 1 tsp sweet paprika powder (plus a pinch of chili, to taste)
  • 20 g grated parmesan or other hard cheese
  • 1/2 bunch of basil
  • juice of 1 lemon wedge
  • Salt, pepper

Orzo

  • 200 g orzo
  • 150 g cooked chickpeas (drained, about 1/2 can)
  • 1 onion
  • 1 carrot
  • 150 g brown mushrooms, portobello, or other mushrooms
  • 400 ml water
  • 6 g veggie stock powder
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 50 g cream cheese or crème fraîche

Halloumi & topping

  • 200 g halloumi
  • 1 tsp oil
  • 50 g cream cheese or crème fraîche
  • Remaining basil
  • Lemon wedges

Instructions

  1. Roast peppers: Preheat the oven to 200 °C top/bottom heat (180 °C fan) and boil the water in a kettle. Halve and deseed the peppers and cut each half into 3 pieces. Put them into a baking dish, press in the garlic, toss with olive oil, salt and pepper and roast for 10–12 minutes until soft.
  2. Hidden veggies: Finely dice the onion. Peel the carrot and coarsely grate it together with the mushrooms. Drain and rinse the chickpeas. Mix the hot water with the stock powder. Heat the oil in a large pan and sweat the onion for about 2 minutes without browning.
  3. Orzo: Add the grated vegetables and sweat for another 3–4 minutes. Add the orzo, chickpeas and stock, cover and simmer over medium heat for 12–15 minutes until the orzo is cooked, stirring occasionally. Cut the lemon into 6 wedges.
  4. Pine nuts & basil: Toast the pine nuts in a dry pan for 2–3 minutes until golden, then set aside (keep the pan for the halloumi). Pick the basil leaves.
  5. Pesto: Blend the roasted peppers and garlic with the toasted pine nuts (keep a few for topping), paprika powder, half of the basil, the cheese and the juice of 1 lemon wedge until smooth. Season with salt, pepper and more lemon juice. Stir the pesto and half of the cream cheese into the cooked orzo and season again.
  6. Halloumi: Cut the halloumi into slices of about 1 cm. Heat the oil in the pan and fry the halloumi over medium-high heat for 2–3 minutes per side until golden brown.
  7. Serve: Cut the remaining basil into thin strips. Divide the orzo onto plates, top with the halloumi, a spoonful of cream cheese, the remaining pine nuts and the basil, and serve with lemon wedges.

Tips

  • Pesto upgrade: A few sun-dried tomatoes make the pesto deeper and richer. Short on time? Use roasted peppers from a jar.
  • Hidden veggies: Zucchini, celeriac or fennel can be grated in as well (or instead of the mushrooms).
  • Cream: Mascarpone makes it extra creamy, ricotta lighter.
  • Halloumi: Drizzle the fried halloumi with a little honey or lemon juice for a sweet-salty contrast.
  • Vegan: Replace the halloumi with crispy fried tofu, use vegan cream cheese and replace the parmesan in the pesto with 1–2 tbsp nutritional yeast.
