Creamy Pumpkin Pasta

Creamy, savoury, lightly spiced pumpkin pasta with a crispy sage walnut topping.

Ingredients

Sage Walnut Topping

  • 100 g walnuts and/or pecan nuts, chopped
  • Fresh sage leaves
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 1 tbsp brown sugar
  • Flaky salt
  • Black pepper to taste

Pasta

  • 500 g pasta (I like rigatoni or penne)
  • 2 tbsp butter
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2-3 shallots, minced
  • Chili flakes to taste
  • 1 tbsp fresh sage, chopped
  • 800 g Hokkaido pumpkin
  • Vegetable broth to thin the sauce
  • Ground nutmeg and cinnamon to taste
  • Ground paprika to taste
  • 150 ml heavy cream
  • 100 g parmesan, finely grated, plus more to serve
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Topping: Chop the Nuts. Melt the butter in a pan over medium-low heat, add sage and fry until crispy. Take out and set aside. In the same pan, fry the garlic, then add nuts and sugar and toast, stirring often, until golden and crisp. Season with salt and pepper and set aside.
  2. Prepare Pumpkin: Cut the pumpkin into chunks, remove seeds and skin. Steam or boil until soft (about 10–15 mins). Purée.
  3. Sauce: Melt the butter in a pan over medium heat. Sauté garlic, shallot, chili flakes and sage for 1–2 mins. Whisk in pumpkin purée and broth and simmer for 5–10 mins. For a creamier and silkier sauce, purée again.
  4. Pasta: Meanwhile, cook the pasta al dente in salted water. Reserve some pasta water before draining.
  5. Finish Sauce: Lower the heat, stir in cream and parmesan and simmer for 1–2 mins. Season with salt, pepper, nutmeg and cinnamon.
  6. Combine: Add the pasta to the sauce. Add pasta water until the sauce is creamy.
  7. Serve: Serve immediately (thickens as it sits) with extra parmesan and the sage walnut topping.
