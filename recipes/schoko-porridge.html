Chocolate Porridge

Breakfast that tastes like dessert.
Warm, creamy and perfect before a long training session (large servings).

Ingredients

Porridge

  • 150 g rolled oats
  • 800 (depending on how thick you like it) ml liquid: oat milk (or any milk)/water or both
  • 20 g cocoa powder
  • 20 g pudding powder (vanilla or chocolate)
  • Sweetener (sugar, maple syrup, stevia), to taste
  • a pinch of salt

Toppings (optional)

  • banana slices, pear or berries
  • peanut butter
  • chocolate, hazelnut cream
  • chopped nuts or cocoa nibs

Instructions

  1. Put oats, milk, water, cocoa powder, pudding powder and salt in a small pot and bring to a boil while stirring.
  2. Simmer on low heat for about 5 minutes, stirring often, until thick and creamy. Add a splash of milk if it gets too thick.
  3. Take off the heat and stir in the sweetener of your choice.
  4. Serve with banana slices, a spoon of peanut butter, some nuts etc.
