Vegan Mushroom Stroganoff

on fusilli with almond-chili topping

Creamy mushroom stroganoff with smoked tofu, paprika, grainy mustard, lemon and fresh herbs,
served with fusilli and crunchy toasted almonds.

Ingredients

Pasta

  • 250 g fusilli
  • 1 tbsp salt for the pasta water

Almond-chili topping

  • 50 g flaked almonds
  • Chili flakes, to taste

Stroganoff

  • 400 g mushrooms (e.g. brown mushrooms, portobello, oyster or king oyster mushrooms)
  • 200 g smoked tofu
  • 1 onion
  • 2 garlic cloves
  • 10 g fresh parsley and thyme
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 1 tbsp vegan margarine
  • 1 tsp sweet or smoked paprika powder
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 100 ml pasta water
  • 250 ml plant-based cooking cream (of your choice, e.g., soy, oat, spelt or almond)
  • 1 tbsp grainy mustard
  • 4 g veggie stock powder
  • juice of 0.33 lemon
  • Salt, pepper

To serve

  • Lemon wedges

Instructions

  1. Prep: Boil water with the salt. Cut the lemon into 6 wedges and coarsely dice the onion. Slice large mushrooms about 0.5 cm thick, halve or quarter smaller ones. Cut the smoked tofu into small cubes (about 1 cm). Pick the herb leaves and chop parsley and thyme separately.
  2. Pasta: Cook the fusilli for 7–8 minutes until al dente. Reserve 100 ml of the pasta water, then drain. Return the pasta to the pot, keep warm.
  3. Topping: Toast the flaked almonds with chili flakes (to taste) in a large dry pan for 2–3 minutes until golden. Set aside.
  4. Tofu & mushrooms: Heat oil and 1 tbsp margarine in the same pan and fry the tofu cubes for 3–4 minutes until crispy, then push them to the side. Add the mushrooms and onion and fry for 6–8 minutes until nicely browned (don't stir too often and don't overcrowd the pan, otherwise they release water and steam instead of browning. If needed, fry in two batches). Add thyme and half of the parsley, press in the garlic, dust with the paprika powder and fry for another 3–4 minutes.
  5. Sauce: Deglaze with the soy sauce and the reserved pasta water. Stir in stock powder, cooking cream, mustard, the juice of 2 lemon wedges and a pinch of salt. Cover and simmer for 5–7 minutes, then season with salt and pepper.
  6. Serve: Divide the fusilli onto deep plates and add the stroganoff. Sprinkle with the almond-chili topping and the remaining parsley and serve with lemon wedges.

Tips

  • Extra umami: Add a dash of white wine when deglazing, or stir 1 tsp miso paste into the sauce.
  • Tofu: Instead of frying the smoked tofu cubes with the mushrooms, you can top the stroganoff with crispy fried tofu slices.
  • Base: Instead of fusilli, try tagliatelle, rice, mashed potatoes or buckwheat.
