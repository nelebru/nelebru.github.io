Apple Crumble Sheet Cake

Makes a whole baking tray (about 25 pieces). Best eaten still warm.

Ingredients

Apples

  • 7 large apples (best: Boskoop <3)
  • juice of 1/4 organic lemon
  • Some vanilla sugar/extract

Base

  • 150 g butter, softened
  • 125 g sugar
  • 2 eggs
  • 1 packet vanilla sugar
  • 300 g flour (type 405)
  • 15 g baking powder
  • zest of 1/4 - 1/2 organic lemon
  • 150 g joghurt
  • a generous pinch of salt

Cinnamon crumble

  • 350 g flour (type 405)
  • 230 g butter
  • 230 g sugar
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 1 packet vanilla sugar
  • another generous pinch of salt

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 175°C (top/bottom heat).
  2. Apples: Peel, quarter and core the apples, cut them into thin slices and toss with the lemon juice and vanilla.
  3. Base: Cream together butter, sugar, eggs and vanilla sugar. Mix in flour, baking powder, lemon zest, yogurt, and salt until smooth.
  4. Line a baking tray (30x40cm) with parchment paper and spread the base evenly over it (it is quite thin, yes).
  5. Crumble: Mix together all ingredients until crumbly.
  6. Assemble: Spread the apple slices evenly over the base, then sprinkle the crumble on top.
  7. Bake: Bake for about 40 minutes. If the crumble gets too dark, cover it with parchment paper for the last third of the baking time.
  8. Let cool briefly.
  9. Best eaten still warm :)

Tips

  • Baking directly on the buttered tray (without parchment paper) makes the base crispier.
  • Optional: knead 150 g marzipan into the crumble and/or sprinkle some chopped almonds on top.
