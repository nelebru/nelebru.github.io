Pumpkin Softies

Fluffy, soft, slightly sweet pumpkin buns. Makes 8 buns. Can be made in advance and frozen.
Warmly spiced with cinnamon, cardamom, and nutmeg.
For all you Lübeckers who love the Junge Softies - this is for you! <3

Ingredients

Dough

  • 250 g Hokkaido pumpkin
  • 100 g milk
  • 400 g light wheat flour
  • 15 g fresh yeast
  • 50 g brown sugar
  • 1 tsp sugar
  • 60 g butter
  • 2 g salt
  • 1 tsp cinnamon
  • 2 pinches (freshly grated) nutmeg
  • 2 pinches cardamom (to taste)

Egg wash

  • 1 egg yolk
  • 1 tbsp milk

Instructions

  1. Pumpkin: Cut the pumpkin into cubes/slices and cook it.
  2. Dough: In the meantime, dissolve the yeast and 1 tsp sugar in the lukewarm milk.
  3. Fold in flour, salt, sugar, cinnamon, nutmeg and cardamom.
  4. Purée: Purée the drained pumpkin (I like to have some texture in it, so I don’t purée it completely smooth)
  5. Knead & rise: Add the butter and the warm pumpkin purée (on top, so that the butter melts), knead into a smooth dough. It should not be sticky. Add some milk or flour if necessary. Cover and let rise in a warm place for about 30 minutes.
  6. Shape: Line the baking dish with parchment paper (or don't - I usually use a glass baking dish without parchment paper). Divide the dough into 8 equal pieces. Shape each into a ball by rolling it under your cupped hand with light pressure until the top is smooth and domed.
  7. Second rise: Place the buns in the dish, cover and let rise in a warm place for another 30 minutes. Meanwhile, preheat the oven to 180°C (top/bottom heat).
  8. Bake: Whisk the egg yolk with the milk and brush the buns with it. Bake on the middle rack for 20–25 minutes until golden brown. Cover with parchment paper if they brown too quickly (usually I don't have to).
