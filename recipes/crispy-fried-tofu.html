Crispy Fried Tofu

glazed with soy sauce

Thin tofu slices coated in plant milk and flour, fried until crispy and glazed with soy sauce.
A quick topping for bowls, pasta, salads, e.g. the orzo with Calabrese pesto.

Ingredients

  • 200 g firm tofu (I prefer plain tofu)
  • 50 ml plant milk (e.g. soy or oat)
  • 3 tbsp flour
  • Salt, pepper
  • 2 tbsp oil
  • Enough soy sauce for deglazing
  • Optional: paprika powder, garlic powder, chili flakes, maple syrup or brown sugar for seasoning

Instructions

  1. Slice: Drain the tofu and cut it into slices of about 0.5 cm.
  2. Coat: Pour the plant milk into a deep plate. Mix the flour with a pinch of salt and pepper on a second plate. Dip each tofu slice into the milk first, then turn it in the flour until coated on all sides.
  3. Fry: Heat the oil in a large pan over medium-high heat. Fry the tofu slices for 3–4 minutes per side until golden and crispy.
  4. Glaze: Deglaze with the soy sauce and toss briefly until it has evaporated and coats the tofu. Serve right away.

Tips

  • Seasoning: Add paprika powder, garlic powder or a pinch of chili to the flour.
  • Sweet-savory: Mix the soy sauce with 1 tsp maple syrup or sprinkle some brown sugar over the tofu while frying for a sweet-savory glaze.
