Coconut Rice Bowl

Creamy coconut rice with crispy sesame sweet potato,
garlic-chili sugar snap peas, cucumber salad and avocado.

Ingredients

Rice

  • 180 g jasmine rice
  • 250 ml coconut milk
  • 150 ml water
  • a pinch of salt

Soy dressing

  • 25 ml soy sauce
  • juice of 0.5 organic lime
  • 2 tbsp sugar

Bowl

  • 150 g sugar snap peas
  • 1 sweet potato
  • 50 g sesame seeds
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 chili
  • 1 cucumber
  • 1 large carrot
  • 1 avocado
  • Some oil
  • Salt, pepper

To serve

  • Some sweet chili sauce
  • Some lime wedges

Instructions

  1. Preheat: Preheat the oven to 220°C top/bottom heat (200°C fan).
  2. Dressing & sweet potato: Stir soy sauce, lime juice and sugar into a dressing. Peel the sweet potato and cut into 2 cm cubes. Toss with the sesame seeds, 1 tbsp of the dressing and 1 tbsp oil. Spread on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper and bake for 20–25 minutes until golden brown.
  3. Coconut rice: Meanwhile, rinse the rice with cold water. Bring it to a boil with coconut milk, water and a pinch of salt, then cover and simmer on low heat for about 12 minutes. Take off the heat and let it rest covered for a few more minutes.
  4. Garlic & chili: Finely chop the garlic. Deseed the chili, finely chop one half and cut the other half into thin rings. Put 1/3 of the garlic into a bowl, the other 2/3 with the chopped chili into a large pan.
  5. Cucumber-carrot salad & avocado: Cut the cucumber into 1 cm thick sticks. Peel the carrot and cut it into thin strips (or shave it into ribbons with a peeler). Add both to the garlic in the bowl, toss with 3 tbsp of the dressing and season with salt and pepper. Let marinate while you cook the rest. Slice the Avocado into strips and drizzle with a little lime juice.
  6. Snap peas: Trim the sugar snap peas. Heat 1 tbsp oil in the pan with the garlic and chili, add the snap peas with a splash of water and fry for about 5 minutes until tender-crisp. Season with salt and pepper.
  7. Serve: Fluff the rice with a fork and divide into deep bowls. Top with sweet potato, snap peas, cucumber-carrot salad and avocado and sprinkle with the chili rings. Serve with the remaining dressing, sweet chili sauce and lime wedges.
