Carrot Cake with Cream Cheese Frosting

Moist German-style carrot cake with creamy frosting.
Prep 30 mins, bake 50-60 mins.

Ingredients

    Cake

  • 375 g carrots
  • 250 g flour
  • 275 g sugar
  • 1 pkt vanilla sugar
  • 3 tsp baking powder
  • 1 pinch salt
  • 1/2 tsp cinnamon
  • 250 g neutral oil
  • 4 eggs
  • 200 g almonds

    • Cream Cheese Frosting

  • 200 g cream cheese (full fat)
  • 2 tbsp cream
  • 100 g powdered sugar

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 150°C. Grease a 26cm springform pan.
  2. Grind 100g almonds finely, chop 100g almonds coarsely. Grate carrots. Make 100g sugar into powdered sugar and set aside.
  3. Beat 275g sugar, vanilla sugar, eggs and oil until creamy (2 minutes).
  4. Mix in flour, baking powder, salt and cinnamon.
  5. Fold in almonds and grated carrots.
  6. Pour batter into prepared pan and bake 50-60 minutes until a toothpick comes out clean.
  7. Cool completely.
  8. Beat cream cheese with cream and powdered sugar until smooth. Spread over cooled cake.

Tip: Makes 18 muffins. Bake in lined muffin tins for 35-40 minutes at 150°C, frost when cooled.
