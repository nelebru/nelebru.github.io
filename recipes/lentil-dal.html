Red Lentil Dal

Quick, creamy one-pot dal with red lentils, tomatoes, coconut milk and garam masala.
Makes a large batch that can be frozen in portions. Serve with rice or my favourite naan.

Ingredients

Dal

  • 400 g red lentils
  • 3 onions, finely diced
  • 3 garlic cloves, finely diced
  • 1-2 tbsp olive oil
  • Vegetable broth + water to reach the desired consistency.
  • 800 g canned, diced tomatoes
  • 400-800 ml coconut milk
  • 1 squeeze of lime juice
  • 1 tbsp (or more) garam masala (consists of cinnamon, coriander, cumin, cardamom, cloves and I sometimes add more cinnamon)
  • 1 tsp turmeric / curry powder
  • Salt and black pepper to taste

Toppings

  • Fresh coriander, chopped
  • Roasted cashews
  • Yogurt of your choice

Instructions

  1. Sauté: Heat the oil in a pot and sweat the onion and garlic. Add part (1/2) of the lentils and sweat for another 2 mins.
  2. Simmer: Add broth, coconut milk and tomatoes. Cover and simmer on low heat. Add the rest of the lentils after some time (few minutes). Add more water to reach the desired consistency. Stir occasionally.
  3. Season: Stir in lime juice, garam masala, salt and pepper and let it simmer. In the end, the lentils should still hava a little bite.
  4. Serve: Top with coriander, roasted cashews and some yogurt.
