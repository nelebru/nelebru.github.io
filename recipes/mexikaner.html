Mexikaner (Spicy Tomato Shot)

The spicy party shot from Northern Germany, made with tomato juice, Korn (German grain spirit) and Tabasco.
Makes about 1.75 l. Serve ice cold.

Ingredients

  • 1 l tomato juice
  • 500 ml Korn (you can also use Vodka)
  • 250 ml spicy Sangrita (picante)
  • 2 tbsp Tabasco
  • 1 tsp salt
  • 1 tbsp pepper
  • Vegetable stock, to taste
  • Tomato paste, to taste

Instead of Sangrita

  • 150 ml tomato juice
  • 100 ml orange juice
  • 2 tbsp lime juice
  • 1/2 tsp Tabasco
  • A pinch of salt

Instructions

  1. If you don't have Sangrita, mix the substitute ingredients first.
  2. Stir tomato juice, Sangrita, Tabasco, salt, pepper, vegetable stock and tomato paste until everything is dissolved.
  3. Add the Korn, stir again and season to taste.
  4. Chill well in the fridge and serve ice cold as shots.
