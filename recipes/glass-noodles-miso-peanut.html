Miso Peanut Noodles

with broccoli

Glass noodles in creamy miso peanut sauce with broccoli, pointed pepper and mushrooms,
topped with a sweet-spicy sesame peanut crunch.

Ingredients

Noodles

  • 150 g glass noodles (or the same amount of dried rice noodles or soba)
  • or 400 g fresh/pre-cooked udon

Miso peanut sauce

  • 50 g peanut butter
  • 25 g white miso paste
  • 1 tbsp soy sauce
  • 1 garlic clove
  • 1 thumb-sized piece of ginger (about 10 g)
  • juice of 0.5 lime
  • 150 ml hot water
  • 2 tsp sugar
  • 4 g veggie stock powder
  • Pepper

Sesame peanut topping

  • 10 g sesame seeds
  • 40 g roasted peanuts
  • Mild chili flakes, to taste
  • 0.5 tsp sugar
  • 0.5 tbsp oil
  • Salt, pepper

Vegetables

  • 250 g broccoli
  • 100 g red pointed pepper
  • 150 g brown mushrooms
  • 25 g spring onion
  • 100 g edamame, shelled (optional, frozen is fine)
  • 1 tbsp oil
  • 100 ml water

To serve

  • Remaining lime wedges
  • Optional: crispy fried tofu

Instructions

  1. Prep: Boil water. Slice the white and green parts of the spring onion into thin rings, keeping them separate. Halve and deseed the pointed pepper and cut it into 1 cm strips. Cut the lime into quarters, halve or quarter the mushrooms and cut the broccoli into florets.
  2. Noodles: Put the glass noodles into a large bowl, cover with boiling water and let them soak for 5–6 minutes. Drain, rinse briefly with cold water and let drain. (Cook other noodles according to the package instructions.)
  3. Sauce: Peel and finely grate garlic and ginger. In a bowl, whisk peanut butter, miso paste, soy sauce, garlic, ginger, the juice of 2 lime wedges, sugar and stock powder with the hot water until smooth, and season with pepper. No need to cook it, it thickens later in the pan.
  4. Topping: Heat the oil in a large pan. Add sesame seeds, peanuts, chili flakes (to taste), sugar, salt and pepper and toast for 2–3 minutes while stirring constantly. Remove from the pan and set aside.
  5. Vegetables: Heat the oil in the same pan and fry the broccoli with the white spring onion rings for 5–6 minutes until lightly browned. Deglaze with the hot water and cook for another 2–3 minutes. Add pepper strips, mushrooms and edamame (if using) and fry for another 4–6 minutes until the vegetables are tender.
  6. Serve: Reduce the heat, pour in the sauce and fold in the noodles. Toss for 1–2 minutes until the sauce thickens slightly and coats everything (add a splash of water if it gets too thick or doesn't coat all the noodles). Divide onto plates, sprinkle with the green spring onion rings and the sesame peanut topping, and serve with the remaining lime wedges and, if you like, crispy fried tofu.
