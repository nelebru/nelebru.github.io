Tortellini Broccoli Bake

Creamy tortellini bake with broccoli.

Ingredients

  • 800 g fresh tortellini with spinach/ricotta filling
  • 1-2 head(s) of broccoli (ratio to taste)
  • 3 carrots (optional)
  • 70 g veggie ham (optional)
  • 70 g parmesan
  • 130 g grated cheese of your choice for topping (e.g. mozzarella, gouda or a mix)
  • 1/2 bunch parsley
  • 1 onion
  • 3 garlic cloves
  • 1 tbsp olive oil
  • 700 ml milk
  • 2-3 tbsp butter
  • 2-3 tbsp flour
  • A pinch of nutmeg
  • Salt and pepper to taste

Instructions

  1. Tortellini: Spread the fresh tortellini in a baking dish.
  2. Prep: Preheat the oven to 200 °C (fan). Cut the broccoli into small florets. Cut the carrots into small cubes. Dice the veggie ham, grate the parmesan and chop the parsley.
  3. Sauce: Dice the onion and mince the garlic. Sauté both in olive oil until translucent. Add the broccoli and fry briefly.
  4. Prepare Sauce: Melt the butter in a saucepan, add the flour and stir to form a roux. Gradually add the milk while whisking to avoid lumps. Season with salt, pepper and nutmeg.
  5. Finish Sauce: Pour the sauce over the broccoli and stir in the veggie ham, parmesan and parsley.
  6. Bake: Pour the sauce over the tortellini, sprinkle with cheese of your choice and bake for approx. 25 mins until golden.
  7. Serve: Garnish with the remaining parsley and serve :)
