### 2. Hosting (GitHub Pages)

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc., 88 Colin P. Kelly Jr. Street,
San Francisco, CA 94107, USA. When you visit the site, GitHub processes your IP address and 
technical request data (date and time, requested page, browser, operating system) in server

Data may be transferred to the USA. GitHub is certified under the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework 
(Art. 45 GDPR). For details, see the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

### 3. Maps on the Cycling page (Leaflet and OpenStreetMap)

The route maps run on Leaflet, which is hosted right here on this site. By default, 
you only see the routes on a plain background, and nothing leaves this website.

Want the real map underneath? Click "Show OpenStreetMap content". Only 
then does your browser load map images from the OpenStreetMap Foundation 
(St John's Innovation Centre, Cowley Road, Cambridge, CB4 0WS, United Kingdom), 
sending them your IP address and the map area you're looking at. More in the [OpenStreetMap Privacy Policy](https://osmfoundation.org/wiki/Privacy_Policy). Changed your mind? Click "Hide OpenStreetMap content" or simply reload the page. Your choice isn't saved anywhere. 


### 4. Links to other websites

This website links to external services such as LinkedIn, GitHub, Google Scholar and ResearchGate. No data is transmitted to these services until you click a link. After that, the privacy policy of the respective service applies.

### 5. Contact by e-mail

If you contact me by e-mail, I can see your e-mail address luckily also and the content of your message to answer your request (Art. 6(1)(b) or (f) GDPR). I delete the data once it is no longer needed. 

### 6. Your rights

You can ask me what data I have about you, have it corrected or deleted, restrict or object to its processing, or get a copy of it (Art. 15–18, 20, 21 GDPR). Just drop me an e-mail.
