I am a triathlete for Tri-Sport Lübeck and a cyclist for the RST Racing Team. I love bikepacking and, more for myself than for others, I created a map to mark all visited roads and trails so far — since this is a Strava pro feature I love and don’t want to lose along with the subscription. Much more to come and mark on this map :)
Here, I share with you some of my favourite courses in my region(s). If you are among the lucky ones living in one of these beautiful regions, have fun exploring them!
